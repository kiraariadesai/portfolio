Kira Aria Desai is a journalist and multimedia storyteller covering the media beat, with a focus on the intersection of technology, media, and society. She reports on the platforms, algorithms, and subcultures shaping how audiences encounter information today, and the cultural ripples that follow.

Her work spans longform reporting, original research, web design, and data storytelling. Recent projects include a literature review and primary-research study on algorithmic radicalization in young users, a coded site exploring fast fashion's environmental cost, and culture writing on hip-hop's influence over the global fashion industry.

Professionally, she has held internships across adtech and banking, at Mediaocean (NYC), where she shaped customer engagement campaigns and analyzed performance metrics, and at Wells Fargo (Charlotte), where she conducted competitive product research for the Affluent Banking division and presented findings to executives.

She is open to roles in audience and social strategy, multimedia and design-forward journalism, and editorial-product work at media organizations that take their audience seriously.
